Physics & Equations

Reference
Gravity & motion
Newton's universal law of gravitation F = G M☉m / r2
Gravitational acceleration of the planet a = −G M☉r / r3 = −μr / r3
Orbit geometry
Ellipse with the Sun at one focus r(ν) = a(1 − e2) / (1 + e cos ν)
Perihelion and aphelion distances rp = a(1 − e),   ra = a(1 + e)
Eccentricity from the apsides e = (ra − rp) / (ra + rp)
Energy & launch conditions
Vis-viva equation v = √( μ(2/r − 1/a) )
Perihelion launch speed vp = √( μ(1 + e) / (a(1 − e)) )
Specific orbital energy ε = v2/2 − μ/r = −μ/(2a)
Kepler's laws
Kepler's third law T = 2π √( a3/μ ) = 2π √( a3/(G M☉) )
Specific angular momentum h = r × v,   h = √( μ a(1 − e2) )
Kepler's second law: constant areal velocity dA/dt = h/2
Area swept in equal time intervals A(Δt) = h Δt / 2
Velocity Verlet integration
Position update rn+1 = rn + vnΔt + ½anΔt2
Velocity update vn+1 = vn + ½(an + an+1)Δt
Fixed time step Δt = T/32,768 ≈ 963 s

Model: ideal two-body Kepler problem with the Sun fixed at a focus. Planet mass, general relativity, perturbations, and radiation pressure are neglected. All calculations use SI units; AU and days are display units.

Numerical method: Velocity Verlet, fixed Δt, 32,768 steps per orbit. Central mass: Sun. Semi-major axis: fixed at 1 AU.

Built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No external services or API keys.
